# study on phone and pc

open **https://nihongo.okiso.net/** on either device. the same web app works on both; no desktop server, account, or native app installation is required.

## textbook entries 180–190

open **https://nihongo.okiso.net/#/study/180-190** for the 11 kanji and 39 words transcribed from these textbook pages. the same set is linked on the study home page. recall a word's reading and meaning, reveal them, then choose **again** or **got it**. missed words return in the round. the queue and revealed answer save on this device and are included in backups; this is self-rated practice, separate from measured writing and spaced-recall evidence.

**writing practice → open** selects that exact range in the saved study flow. the custom range is ready to start; the daily round stays saved separately. new lessons introduce a small useful word set before checking readings, meanings and writing.

## quick reference

the **reference** button stays available during study. it opens the current kanji in a panel with **meaning**, **drawing**, and **words** tabs. search by character, number, reading, or english meaning to look up another kanji. drawing includes stroke-order replay, guided tracing, and a blank pad.

**back to study** or **escape** returns to the same step and typed answer. timed practice pauses while the panel is open. reference practice does not advance the lesson or earn recall credit; an answer exposed during a check is recorded as help.

## choose your own study or the daily path

**my own study** opens first. enter your start and end numbers, then click **study 191–200** or press enter. this includes all ten characters, in book order, with no daily limit. you can also choose a textbook chapter.

**daily path** is optional: two characters at a time, with a separate adjustable range and due reviews. switching between modes preserves both sessions. all 300 character ids and the printed book order stay intact.

each character has three phases:

1. **learn:** main meanings and two or three useful words with readings and meanings. **more words** expands optional examples. entries 191–200 have hand-picked school/everyday words; other entries use the textbook/common-word ordering, not a measured frequency chart.
2. **practise:** draw as much as you want on the free pad, with undo, clear and an optional model. guided tracing can be repeated without advancing. meanings and the word list remain available. choose **i’m ready · test me** when you want to move on.
3. **check:** type the hidden reading and meaning for each taught word, then write the character from its cue. these checks use the same words shown in the lesson. a missed answer offers **learn & practise again** while preserving the recorded result.

custom study finishes these phases for one character before moving to the next. the daily path uses the same phases in its smaller rounds. a compound can recur when learning each of its characters. same-visit work is practice; a summary records what happened without claiming lasting memory.

the round saves before it advances. close it, reopen the site, and continue your saved range or daily round. the current learning phase, feedback, help and committed paper/self-check stages also survive reload. free-pad ink is temporary and clears on reload; it is not a scored attempt. starting a different custom range keeps completed answers and leaves the daily round intact.

## writing

on phone, draw with a finger or stylus. on pc, use a mouse/pen or choose **on paper**. only the drawing surface blocks scrolling; the rest of the page remains scrollable. the outline/model is hidden during the screen check. requesting an outline, or receiving an automatic stroke hint, marks that attempt as helped.

the screen checker compares ordered strokes to the bundled model and reports slips. it does not assess handwriting like a teacher. paper is explicitly self-reported: write first, reveal the model, then compare. paper practice cannot silently become an automatically verified screen-writing result.

the library's reference pad remains available. **study this character with saved recall** opens a focused range for the selected character.

## later reviews

word checks are saved separately by word and reading, so a new compound does not overwrite another compound’s evidence. due reviews use that same word with the answer hidden and check one skill per character, so one answer does not immediately prime the next check for that same character. recent model exposure counts as practice and cannot increase a spaced-recall streak. word reading, writing, semantic checks and paper self-report remain separate. repeated same-day practice never creates a later-recall claim.

the older kanji practice and optional play room remain available. their scores and stage clears are separate from the study round's evidence. the practice grid's highest tier requires all core skills plus spaced checks.

## grammar

choose **grammar**, search by topic or lesson, and select **revise**. revision stays within that lesson's patterns. make a sentence before revealing a model. the model is one valid example, not the only accepted sentence; the comparison is clearly self-rated. a passed multiple-choice quiz records lesson completion and introduces patterns for review, rather than certifying independent production of every pattern.

the current lessons are available without identifying a textbook. their ordering is not claimed to match the learner's grammar book.

## install and use offline

open the site online first and allow its service worker to finish downloading the app, fonts, content and local stroke data. use your browser's **install** or **add to home screen** action if you want an app icon. installation is optional. when a new version is ready, a refresh notice lets you update after the session; updates do not erase progress or force a mid-session reload.

offline study needs that successful initial download. device speech/listening depends on an available voice and may behave differently offline. physical-device installation and finger/stylus comfort still need testing on the learner's actual device.

## move between devices

progress is local to each browser/device. in **settings**, export a backup, transfer the JSON file to the other device, and import it there. the confirmation explains that importing **replaces** that device's progress; it does not merge two diverged histories. keep a backup of both before replacing either. clearing browser data can remove progress, so export first.

old backups remain supported. new study records are validated, including partial rounds and explicit help/paper/self-check data. no automatic sync, account or additional cloud service has been added.
